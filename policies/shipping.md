# Shipping and Delivery

Standard shipping takes **3–5 business days** within Australia and is **free on
orders over $35**. Under $35, standard shipping is a flat $6.95.

Express shipping is **1–2 business days** for $12.95 and is available on all
in-stock titles ordered before 2pm AEST.

Tracking details are emailed when an order leaves our warehouse. Parcels are
carried by AusPost and can be tracked with the tracking number on the order.

We currently ship to Australia and New Zealand only. International orders
outside those two countries cannot be placed.

If a parcel is marked delivered but has not arrived, wait 48 hours — carriers
occasionally scan early — then contact support and we will open an enquiry.
