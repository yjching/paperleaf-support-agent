# Returns and Refunds

Physical books may be returned within **30 days of delivery** — eligibility
doesn't depend on the reason. Items must be in resalable condition; opened or
read copies are assessed case by case and may be refused.

A flat **$6.99** AusPost return shipping fee applies to every return
regardless of reason.

We still ask why you're returning something, because it changes what we offer:

- **Damaged or misprinted:** returnable within **30 days of delivery**. No
  need to send the item back — keep or discard the damaged copy, no label
  required. Choose a free replacement copy, or a refund as store credit or
  back to your original payment method.
- **Any other reason (wrong edition, changed your mind, arrived late,
  etc.):** the standard return — a prepaid label is generated for drop-off.

**Refunds are issued in full once the returned item is received and
inspected at our warehouse** — not at drop-off.

**Digital books (ebooks and audiobooks) cannot be returned once they have been
downloaded.** If a download has failed or the file is corrupt, contact support
and we will re-issue it at no cost.

Refunds are issued to the original payment method unless store credit is
requested. Returns over **$150** are reviewed by a member of our team before
the refund is released.

Orders that have not yet been delivered cannot be returned — they can be
cancelled instead while they are still in transit.

We do not offer edition or format swaps as a return outcome — a return is
either a refund or, for damaged/misprinted items, a replacement copy of the
same edition.
