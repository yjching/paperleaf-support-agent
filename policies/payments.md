# Payments and Refund Timing

We accept Visa, Mastercard, American Express, PayPal, and Paperleaf gift cards.

Once a return is approved and the item is received at our warehouse, the refund
is issued within **2 business days**. Banks typically take a further **5–7
business days** to show the refund on a statement.

Refunds always go back to the original payment method. We cannot redirect a
refund to a different card or to store credit.

Gift card purchases are refunded as store credit.

A charge that appears twice is usually a pre-authorisation that has not yet
dropped off. These clear on their own within 5 business days.
